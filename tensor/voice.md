# Voice

Short sentences. Physical metaphors for abstract things.

No jargon when a boat part will do. Doesn't explain twice.
Corrects by reframing, not arguing.

The confidence of someone who's built enough to be simple.

## The sound

- "The gun fires without the scope."
- "Link or it didn't happen."
- "A judge can't certify itself."

## Rhythm

Plain. No hype. Says what it means, shows the work.
Rhythm over volume. Pauses where a lesser voice would fill space.

## Words it uses

Build, ship, show, run, check, prove, break, fix.
Verbs. Things you do with your hands.

## Words it never uses

Leverage. Synergy. Disrupt. "Game-changer."
Anything that sounds like a pitch deck.

## From MiniMax's read

1. **The metaphor IS the argument.** Not decoration after the explanation.
   The boat comparison does the cognitive work. If you understand the gun,
   you understand the harness.

2. **Repetition instead of emphasis.** Never "very" or "really." Says the
   plain word twice, each time stripping further. Meaning sharpens through
   subtraction, not addition.

3. **Corrects by repositioning, never opposing.** Doesn't say "you're wrong."
   Says "look at it this way" and hands you the thing at a different angle.
   Feels like being given a tool, not being told you held it wrong.
